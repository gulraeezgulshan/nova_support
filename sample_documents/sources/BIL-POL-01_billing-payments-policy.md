# Billing and Payments Policy

Document Type: policy
Version: 1.6
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Expiry Date: 2027-12-31
Owner: Billing & Payments

## 1 Purpose

This policy covers payments, charges, invoices and billing disputes.

## 2 Payment Methods

VoltHaven accepts credit and debit cards, PayPal and approved buy-now-pay-later providers. Payment is authorised at checkout and captured when the order is dispatched.

## 3 Duplicate Charges

When a customer reports being charged twice for the same order, the agent must verify the transaction in the payment system. A confirmed duplicate capture is reversed within 5 business days of verification. An authorisation hold that was never captured is released automatically by the bank and is not a duplicate charge; the agent explains this to the customer. Agents must not promise the date the bank will post the reversal. Duplicate charges above USD 500 are reviewed by a supervisor.

## 4 Incorrect Charges

If the amount charged differs from the order total shown at checkout, the agent verifies the order and the transaction. A confirmed overcharge is corrected by refunding the difference to the original payment method.

## 5 Disputes and Chargebacks

Billing disputes over USD 1,000 require Department Manager review. If a customer has opened a chargeback with their bank, the agent must not issue a separate refund for the same amount while the chargeback is open.

## 6 Invoices

Invoices are available in the customer's account. Corrected invoices are issued within 3 business days of a verified billing correction.
