# Delivery Policy

Document ID: DEL-POL-04
Document Type: policy
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2025-01-01
Expiry Date: 2026-12-31
Owner: Logistics & Delivery

## 1 Purpose

This policy defines VoltHaven Electronics' delivery commitments.

## 2 Delivery Options and Commitments

Standard delivery arrives within 7 business days of dispatch. Express delivery arrives within 3 business days of dispatch.

## 4 Delayed Deliveries

### 4.1 Verification

The agent checks courier tracking before responding to a delivery complaint.

### 4.2 Lost Parcels

A parcel with no tracking movement for 10 business days is treated as lost.

## 5 Compensation

### 5.2 Late Delivery Compensation

If a standard order arrives more than 7 business days late, the customer may receive a store credit of 5% of the order value, up to a maximum of USD 25.
