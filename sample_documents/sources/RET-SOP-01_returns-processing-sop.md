# Returns Processing Procedure

Document ID: RET-SOP-01
Document Type: sop
Version: 1.4
Effective Date: 2026-01-15
Expiry Date: 2027-12-31
Owner: Returns & Refunds

## 1 Return Authorisation

Every return needs a return authorisation linked to the order. Wrong items and dead-on-arrival products are collected free of charge.

## 2 Pickup Scheduling

Return pickups are booked in two-hour windows. If a courier misses a scheduled pickup, the agent apologises and reschedules within 2 business days, offering a drop-off point as an alternative.

## 3 Inspection

Returned items are inspected within 3 business days of arrival at the returns centre. The inspection result and photographs are attached to the order.

## 4 Rejected Returns

If a return is rejected, the customer receives the reason and the inspection photographs. The customer may appeal once; appeals are reviewed by a different inspector.

## 5 Replacements and Missing Parts

Approved replacements and missing accessories are dispatched within 2 business days of approval, with tracking sent to the customer.
