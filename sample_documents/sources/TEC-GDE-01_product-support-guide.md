## 1 Setup

Most setup problems are solved by charging the device fully, updating it to the latest software during setup and signing in with the account used for the purchase. Smart-home devices need a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network during pairing.

## 2 Software and Firmware Updates

If an update fails, the customer restarts the device and retries on a stable Wi-Fi connection with at least 50% battery. If the device will not start after an update, the recovery-mode steps for the model are followed; a device that still cannot start is handled as a warranty repair.

## 3 Connectivity

For Wi-Fi or Bluetooth problems the customer restarts the device and router, forgets and re-pairs the connection and checks for software updates. Interference from other devices and distance from the router are checked before a fault is assumed.

## 4 Escalation to Level 2

If the same problem remains after two troubleshooting sessions, the case is escalated to a Level 2 technical specialist, who may book a diagnostic repair.
