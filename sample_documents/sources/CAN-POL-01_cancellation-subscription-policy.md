# Cancellation and Subscription Policy

Document ID: CAN-POL-01
Document Type: policy
Version: 1.1
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Expiry Date: 2027-12-31
Owner: Billing & Payments

## 1 Order Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled free of charge until they are dispatched. Cancelled orders are refunded in full to the original payment method.

## 2 VoltCare+ Subscription

VoltCare+ is a paid protection plan billed monthly or annually. It extends the warranty to 24 months and adds one accidental-damage claim per year.

## 3 Renewal Notices

Subscribers receive a renewal reminder by e-mail at least 7 days before an annual renewal. Monthly plans renew automatically without a reminder.

## 4 Unwanted Renewal Refunds

A customer who did not intend to renew may request a refund of the renewal payment within 14 days of the renewal date, provided no VoltCare+ claim has been made since the renewal. Requests after 14 days are not eligible, and the subscription is cancelled at the end of the paid period instead.

## 5 Cancelling VoltCare+

Customers can cancel VoltCare+ at any time in their account. Cancellation stops future renewals; the cover remains active until the end of the paid period.
