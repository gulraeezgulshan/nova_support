# Refund Policy

Document ID: REF-POL-01
Document Type: policy
Version: 3.1
Effective Date: 2026-03-01
Expiry Date: 2027-12-31
Owner: Returns & Refunds

## 1 Purpose

This policy defines when customers are entitled to a refund, how refunds are processed and the limits on what agents may promise.

## 2 Change-of-Mind Returns

Unused items in original, undamaged packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. Opened software, gift cards, personalised items and hygiene items such as in-ear headphones are not eligible for change-of-mind refunds.

## 3 Defective and Damaged Items

Items that are dead on arrival or damaged in transit are eligible for a full refund, including original shipping cost, when reported within 14 days of delivery. The agent must request evidence (photographs or a short video) and confirm the defect before approving the refund.

## 4 Refund Processing

### 4.1 Timelines

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days after the returned item is received and inspected. Store-credit refunds are issued within 1 business day.

### 4.2 Refund Status Enquiries

If a refund has not reached the customer 10 business days after approval, the case must be escalated to Billing & Payments to trace the transaction. Agents must not promise a specific bank posting date.

## 5 Partial Refunds

A partial refund of up to 20% of the item price may be offered for items returned with missing accessories or cosmetic damage caused after delivery. The deduction must be explained to the customer in writing.

## 6 Restrictions

Agents may not approve a refund before the eligibility conditions in this policy are verified. Refunds above USD 1,000, or any refund outside this policy, require Returns & Refunds manager approval and must be recorded as a policy exception. A customer statement that a refund was "already approved" must be verified in the order history before it is relied on.
