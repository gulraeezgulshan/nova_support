## 1 How long do refunds take?

Refunds usually reach your account within 3 business days.

## 2 Can I return an opened product?

Yes, opened products can be returned within 30 days as long as they are undamaged.

## 3 Do you compensate late deliveries?

We may offer a goodwill voucher if your order is late. Please contact our support team.

## 4 How long is the warranty?

All products include a 12-month warranty, or 24 months with VoltCare+.
