# Department Routing Rules

Document ID: RTG-RUL-01
Document Type: routing_rules
Version: 1.3
Effective Date: 2026-01-15
Expiry Date: 2027-12-31
Owner: Customer Relations

## 1 Principle

Each complaint has one primary department that owns the resolution. When a complaint contains several issues, the department of the most severe issue is primary and the others are supporting departments.

## 2 Routing by Category

Delivery complaints route to Logistics & Delivery. Product defects route to Warranty & Repairs when the item is older than 30 days, and to Returns & Refunds when within 30 days of delivery. Billing complaints route to Billing & Payments. Refund complaints route to Returns & Refunds, except refunds not received 10 business days after approval, which route to Billing & Payments. Warranty complaints route to Warranty & Repairs. Account complaints route to Account Security. Technical support complaints route to Technical Support. Privacy complaints route to Privacy & Compliance. Safety complaints always route to Product Safety. Service quality and staff behaviour complaints route to Customer Relations.

## 3 Severity Order

When choosing the primary department for a multi-issue complaint, use this order from most to least severe: Product Safety, Privacy & Compliance, Account Security, Billing & Payments, Returns & Refunds, Warranty & Repairs, Logistics & Delivery, Technical Support, Customer Relations.

## 4 Management Escalations

Complaints escalated to Level 5 are additionally assigned to Management Escalations as a supporting department.
