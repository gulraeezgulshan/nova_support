# Customer Service Level Agreement

Document ID: SLA-AGR-01
Document Type: sla
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Expiry Date: 2027-12-31
Owner: Customer Relations

## 1 Priority Definitions

P0 Critical covers safety, security and privacy incidents. P1 High covers legal threats, high-value disputes and repeated unresolved complaints. P2 Medium covers standard product, delivery, billing and refund issues. P3 Low covers general questions and minor service feedback.

## 2 Response and Resolution Targets

P0 Critical: first response within 30 minutes, resolution within 4 hours. P1 High: first response within 2 hours, resolution within 24 hours. P2 Medium: first response within 8 hours, resolution within 3 days. P3 Low: first response within 24 hours, resolution within 7 days.

## 3 At-Risk Monitoring

A complaint is at risk when 50% of the resolution window has elapsed for P0, 70% for P1, 75% for P2 and 80% for P3. At-risk complaints are highlighted to the owning department manager.

## 4 VIP Customers

VIP customers receive the same priority rules as other customers. Priority is based on business impact and risk, not on customer status or the emotional tone of the complaint.
