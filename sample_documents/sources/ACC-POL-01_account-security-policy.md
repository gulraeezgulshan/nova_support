# Account Security Policy

Document ID: ACC-POL-01
Document Type: policy
Version: 1.2
Effective Date: 2026-01-10
Expiry Date: 2027-12-31
Owner: Account Security

## 1 Purpose

This policy protects customer accounts, payment details and order history from unauthorised use, and defines how account access problems are resolved.

## 2 Identity Verification

Before discussing or changing an account, the agent must verify the customer's identity with the registered e-mail address plus one of: the last four digits of the payment card used, a recent order number, or a one-time code sent to the registered phone number. Agents must never ask for a full card number or password.

## 3 Locked Accounts

Accounts are locked automatically after five failed sign-in attempts. A locked account is unlocked only after identity verification, and the customer is asked to set a new password. Locked-account complaints must be resolved within one business day.

## 4 Unauthorised Access

When a customer reports account activity they did not make, the agent must immediately secure the account by locking it and revoking all active sessions, open an account security incident and escalate for Compliance Review (Level 4). Orders placed without authorisation are cancelled if not yet dispatched. Refunds for unauthorised orders are issued only after the security investigation confirms the activity was not the customer's.

## 5 Customer Guidance

Customers are advised to use a unique password, enable two-step verification and never share one-time codes. VoltHaven staff never ask for passwords or one-time codes by phone, chat or e-mail.
