# Delivery Policy

Document ID: DEL-POL-04
Document Type: policy
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Expiry Date: 2027-12-31
Owner: Logistics & Delivery

## 1 Purpose

This policy defines VoltHaven Electronics' delivery commitments, how delivery problems are handled and when customers are eligible for delivery compensation.

## 2 Delivery Options and Commitments

Standard delivery arrives within 5 business days of dispatch. Express delivery arrives within 2 business days of dispatch. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays. The committed delivery date shown at checkout is the reference date for all delay calculations.

## 3 Order Tracking

Every shipment receives a tracking number by e-mail within 24 hours of dispatch. Agents must check the courier tracking status before responding to any delivery complaint.

## 4 Delayed Deliveries

### 4.1 Verification

A delivery is delayed when it has not arrived by the committed delivery date. The agent must verify the shipment status and confirm a revised delivery date with the courier before promising any date to the customer.

### 4.2 Lost Parcels

A parcel with no tracking movement for 7 consecutive business days is treated as lost. For a lost parcel the customer may choose a free replacement (subject to stock) or a full refund of the item and shipping cost. A courier investigation must be opened for every lost parcel.

## 5 Compensation

### 5.1 Eligibility

Compensation applies only to delays caused by VoltHaven or its couriers. Delays caused by an incorrect address supplied by the customer, a failed delivery attempt where the customer was unavailable, or customs inspection are not eligible.

### 5.2 Late Delivery Compensation

If a standard order arrives more than 5 business days after the committed delivery date, the customer is eligible for a store credit of 10% of the order value, up to a maximum of USD 50. If an express order arrives after its committed date, the express shipping fee is refunded in full.

### 5.3 Limits

Agents may not offer cash compensation for delivery delays. Compensation above the limits in section 5.2 requires Customer Relations manager approval and is treated as a policy exception.

## 6 Damaged in Transit

Items damaged in transit must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photographs of the item and packaging. Verified transit damage qualifies for a free replacement or a full refund under the Refund Policy (REF-POL-01).

## 7 Wrong Address Deliveries

If VoltHaven delivered to an address other than the one on the order, the case is handled as a lost parcel under section 4.2. If the customer supplied the wrong address, the agent may help redirect the parcel but no compensation applies.
