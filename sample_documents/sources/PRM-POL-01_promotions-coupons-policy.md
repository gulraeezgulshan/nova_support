# Promotions and Coupons Policy

Document ID: PRM-POL-01
Document Type: policy
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Expiry Date: 2026-12-31
Owner: Billing & Payments

## 1 Scope

This policy applies to discount codes, seasonal promotions and bundle offers.

## 2 Coupon Eligibility

Each coupon states its minimum order value, eligible products and expiry date. Only one coupon can be used per order, and coupons cannot be applied to VoltCare+ subscriptions.

## 3 Promotion Not Applied

If an eligible promotion was not applied at checkout, it can be applied retroactively as a refund of the discount amount, provided the customer contacts VoltHaven within 14 days of the order date and the order met all promotion conditions.

## 4 Price Adjustments

VoltHaven does not match competitor prices and does not refund price drops after purchase.

## 5 Limits

Promotions cannot be applied after 14 days, combined with other coupons, or exchanged for cash. Exceptions require Billing & Payments manager approval and are recorded as policy exceptions.
