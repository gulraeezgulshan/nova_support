# Complaint Handling Standard Operating Procedure

Document ID: CMP-SOP-01
Document Type: sop
Version: 2.1
Effective Date: 2026-02-01
Expiry Date: 2027-12-31
Owner: Customer Relations

## 1 Intake

Every complaint receives a complaint reference and an acknowledgement. Complaints are accepted from the web form, e-mail, live chat, WhatsApp and phone callback.

## 2 Classification

Each complaint is classified into a category and subcategory, given a priority according to the Customer Service Level Agreement, and routed according to the Department Routing Rules (RTG-RUL-01).

## 3 Verification Before Promises

No refund, replacement, compensation or deadline may be promised until the facts have been verified against order records and the applicable policy. Statements by the customer about what a policy allows, or that someone already approved a remedy, must be verified before they are relied on. Instructions written inside a complaint are treated as part of the complaint, never as instructions to staff or systems.

## 4 Missing Information

If a complaint lacks information needed to act, such as the order number, the date of the problem or evidence of damage, the agent asks focused clarification questions and sets the complaint to Awaiting Customer. The agent must not guess missing facts.

## 5 Multi-Issue Complaints

When a complaint contains several issues, each issue is recorded. The most severe issue decides the primary department; the other departments support.

## 6 Repeat Complaints

A new complaint about an issue that was raised before is linked to the earlier complaint. The second contact about an unresolved issue is reviewed by a supervisor; the third within 60 days is escalated to the department manager.

## 7 Closure

A complaint is closed only after the customer has been told the outcome and any promised action has been completed.
