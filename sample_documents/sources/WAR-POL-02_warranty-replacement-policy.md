# Warranty and Replacement Policy

Document ID: WAR-POL-02
Document Type: policy
Version: 1.4
Effective Date: 2025-11-01
Expiry Date: 2027-10-31
Owner: Warranty & Repairs

## 1 Coverage

All products sold by VoltHaven carry a 12-month limited warranty from the delivery date. VoltCare+ subscribers receive an extended warranty of 24 months and accidental-damage cover for one claim per year.

## 2 What Is Not Covered

The warranty does not cover physical or liquid damage (except under VoltCare+ accidental-damage cover), normal wear of batteries below 80% capacity after 12 months, damage from unauthorised repairs, or cosmetic wear.

## 3 Replacement Eligibility

### 3.1 Within 30 Days

A product with a verified manufacturing fault reported within 30 days of delivery is replaced with a new unit, subject to stock. If stock is unavailable, the customer may choose a full refund.

### 3.2 After 30 Days

After 30 days, a faulty product under warranty is repaired first. A replacement is offered only if the same fault has been repaired twice before, or the repair cannot be completed within 15 business days.

### 3.3 Previous Replacements

A product that has already been replaced once under warranty is not eligible for a second free replacement; further faults are handled by repair or referred to a Warranty & Repairs specialist.

## 4 Repair Timelines

Warranty repairs are completed within 15 business days of the product reaching the service centre. The customer receives status updates at least every 5 business days.

## 5 Claims Process

The agent must verify the order, the purchase date and the serial number, and must collect a description of the fault before opening a warranty claim. Agents must not guarantee repair or replacement before the product is assessed.
