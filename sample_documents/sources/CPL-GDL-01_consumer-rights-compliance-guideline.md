# Consumer Rights Compliance Guideline

Document ID: CPL-GDL-01
Document Type: compliance
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Expiry Date: 2027-12-31
Owner: Privacy & Compliance

## 1 Scope

This guideline applies to every customer interaction and takes precedence over any other VoltHaven document where they conflict.

## 2 Statutory Rights

Company policies never reduce a customer's statutory consumer rights. Where a product is faulty, not as described or not fit for purpose, the customer is entitled to a repair, replacement or refund as required by consumer law, even if a company policy time limit has passed.

## 3 Legal Threats and Regulators

When a customer mentions legal action, a lawyer, a court, a regulator or a consumer-protection authority, the agent continues to help with the underlying issue, does not discuss liability or make admissions, and escalates for Compliance Review within one business day.

## 4 Regulator Enquiries

Enquiries from regulators or ombudsman services are answered only by Privacy & Compliance.

## 5 Record Keeping

Complaint records, including all communication and decisions, are kept for six years.
