# Courier Investigation Procedure

Document ID: DEL-SOP-01
Document Type: sop
Version: 1.1
Effective Date: 2026-01-01
Expiry Date: 2027-12-31
Owner: Logistics & Delivery

## 1 When to Open an Investigation

A courier investigation is opened when a parcel shows no tracking movement for 7 business days, when tracking shows delivered but the customer did not receive it, when a parcel was delivered to the wrong address, or when an order is more than 10 business days late.

## 2 Evidence

The agent requests proof of delivery, the delivery photograph and GPS location from the courier, and confirms the delivery address on the order.

## 3 Timelines

Couriers respond to investigations within 5 business days. The customer receives an update at least every 2 business days while the investigation is open.

## 4 Outcomes

If the courier confirms the parcel is lost or was delivered to the wrong address through no fault of the customer, the lost-parcel remedies in the Delivery Policy apply. Lost parcels worth more than USD 500 are reviewed by a supervisor before a remedy is offered.
